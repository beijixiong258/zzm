# AIcademiya domain vocabulary

- **Platform account**: a person's sign-in identity across the platform; independent of a course membership. One current global role: Admin, Teacher or Student.
- **Admin**: can administer global accounts and assign active staff to courses. Administrative account powers do not automatically enroll the administrator into every course's teaching content.
- **Teacher**: may co-manage courses to which they are assigned, including those courses' student rosters. Cannot administer global account roles or another course's students.
- **Student**: learns through course enrollment; removing an enrollment is not deletion or disabling of the global account.
- **Course staff assignment**: an explicit association between a course and an Admin or Teacher. A course may have multiple assigned staff. Assigned staff share the course's teaching-management capabilities.
- **Primary course manager**: the responsible staff member used for course ownership continuity. Must remain an active assigned Admin or Teacher. Changing/removing this person's staff role requires an explicit replacement, not silent course deletion.
- **Course enrollment**: the association giving a Student access to a course's published learning content. Course removal and restoration affect that association and its defined retention scope, not unrelated courses.
- **Course population**: active distinct people associated with the current course, grouped by their current global role. Admin/Teacher counts come from course staff assignments; Student count comes from active student enrollments. Never substitute whole-platform user counts.
- **Global User Accounts**: administrator-only account listing, creation/import, role changes and reversible disabling/restoration for Admins, Teachers and Students.
- **Course Students**: course-scoped student management for assigned staff, including creation/enrollment, reviewed import, removal and restoration.
- **Publication / edition**: an immutable student-facing version of course content. A draft metadata edit is not publication. Operational memberships and live population counts are not frozen curriculum content.
- **Document revision**: an immutable representation of one exact source file, with its complete pages, extracted blocks and retained images. A new extraction or source correction creates a new revision; earlier course editions retain the revision they published.
- **Document processing job**: the operational work of turning an uploaded source into a document revision. Completion means the representation was produced, not that every word or visual relationship is accurate.
- **Extraction quality**: a distinction between machine-transcribed content, content needing source review, unsuitable content, and content explicitly approved through source review. A processed page is not a certified-correct page.
- **Source review / correction**: an assigned staff member's explicit check against the original, retaining what was changed and why. It changes the draft document revision, not already-published learning content.
- **Source citation**: a link between an answer's actual supporting quotation and the specific material, document revision, page and block that were read. It does not grant access to otherwise private material.
